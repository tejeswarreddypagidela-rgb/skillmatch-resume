100% Private Free Forever Runs In Browser No Sign-up

What it does

Compares your resume against a job description and shows you where you're a strong match, where you're missing keywords, and how an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is likely to parse your file. It helps you tailor a resume before you hit submit — not promise you an interview.

How it works

Paste a job description, upload a resume (PDF, DOCX, plain text, or even a photo/screenshot), and SkillMatch extracts the text, matches it against a curated skills database, and scores the overlap. Scanned PDFs and images fall back to on-device OCR.

Why it's private

Every step — file parsing, OCR, and skill matching — runs as JavaScript in your browser. There is no server for your resume to go to. Nothing about your job search is logged, tracked, or stored anywhere but your own device.

Who it's for

Job seekers who want a quick, honest gut-check before applying — especially for roles where a keyword-matching ATS is the first filter. Use its output as a starting point, not the final word.
