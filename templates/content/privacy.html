No Tracking No Cookies No Account Local Only

The short version

SkillMatch doesn't collect your data because it can't — there is no backend server for it to go to. Everything, including reading your resume file, runs as JavaScript inside your own browser tab.

What we don't do

  • We don't upload your resume, job description, or any file you provide to any server.
  • We don't use analytics, ad trackers, or third-party scripts.
  • We don't set cookies.
  • We don't require or store an account, email, or name to use the tool.

What is stored locally

SkillMatch uses your browser's localStorage for exactly one thing: remembering whether you prefer light or dark mode. That preference never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The Contact page

The contact form builds an email in your own email client (a mailto: link) — the message is sent directly from your inbox to ours over normal email, the same as if you'd written it yourself. SkillMatch itself never sees or stores that message.

Hosting

This site is hosted as static files on Netlify, which — like any host — may log basic connection metadata (such as IP address and request timing) for operating the service. SkillMatch's own code does not add any tracking on top of that.
